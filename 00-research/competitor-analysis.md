# Competitor analysis: top 5 calorie tracking apps

Based on user reviews (App Store, Google Play, Trustpilot, review aggregators), 2026.

## MyFitnessPal

- Barcode scanner and macro adjustments were removed from the free tier, pushing a paid upgrade
- A recent "Today tab" redesign added taps and made the diary harder to find, with no way to revert
- Database accuracy is inconsistent (crowdsourced); testing found discrepancies in ~23% of entries
- Billing/cancellation friction; support is hard to reach
- Most common complaint: too many taps to log a meal

## YAZIO

- AI photo recognition is inconsistent, often needs manual correction
- Limited micronutrient detail compared to specialized apps
- Database gaps for non-European users
- Free tier has intrusive ads and limited customization
- Rigid fasting-timer UX

## Cronometer

- Frequent complaints about intrusive ads disrupting food logging
- Database considered inadequate outside the US
- Recipe-editing regressions are a top complaint theme
- Benchmark testing: only ~63% of dishes identified correctly, portion size missed by ~±19% on average
- No coaching/accountability layer, pure tracker

## Lose It!

- No natural-language food input; manual search required for every item
- Photo logging is Premium-only and inconsistent
- Previously-free features (macro tracking, barcode scanner for new users) moved behind a paywall
- A redesign made the calendar/history view less accessible
- Community features have drawn criticism for harmful content

## Lifesum

- Very low trust ratings (1.5–2.5 / 5 on Trustpilot)
- Promised curated meal plans are hard or impossible to find after purchase
- Premium seen as offering little over the free tier
- Notable share of complaints about the app failing to launch, especially post-payment
- Slow customer support

## Comparison table

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Core strength | Largest database, social | Clean UI, fasting | Verified micronutrient data | Clean UI, generous free tier | AI meal plans |

## Key opportunity gaps this project addresses

1. **Trustworthy, low-friction logging** — a verified database with fast photo/voice input, always-visible AI confidence, and inline correction (Kalo's Log a meal flow)
2. **Recipes that are actually personalized and reachable** — two clear discovery paths (browse, from my fridge), with results filtered by profile and calorie budget rather than hidden behind a paywall
3. **Honest free tier / pricing transparency** — the subscription screen states exactly what Free vs Plus includes, no bait-and-switch
4. **No hidden subscription traps** — in-app cancellation in one tap, with the exact effective date shown

Tone of voice

Friendly and direct

Kalo speaks like a knowledgeable friend. It states facts, offers the next step, and never judges a choice. No shaming, no diet-culture words, no exclamation-mark cheerleading.

SayYou have 640 kcal left today.
AvoidCareful, you're almost over your limit!
SayLooks like a chicken burrito. Tap to adjust the portion.
AvoidAre you sure you want to log this?
SaySaved. Your ring moved to 72 percent.
AvoidGreat job staying on track, you deserve a cheat meal.
  • Sentences under twelve words. One action per message.
  • Numbers before adjectives. "540 kcal" beats "a light lunch."
  • Words we do not use: cheat, guilty, bad food, burn off, punish, clean.
Principle

Fewest taps to log a meal

Every brand element serves speed. Rings show progress at a glance, big numerals are readable at arm's length, and the primary button is always the only coral thing on the screen.

Color palette

Three accents on warm neutrals

Coral drives, yellow supports, green rewards. Everything else is a warm off-white or ink so food and numbers stand out.

Imagery direction

Flat illustration, plus the user's own photos

Chosen direction: flat, rounded illustration in the brand palette for empty states, onboarding and recipe categories. Real food photos appear only where the user takes them (the logging flow). Why: stock food photography competes with the user's photo and dates fast; abstract shapes alone feel cold for a food app. Flat shapes stay bright, scale to any size, and match the ring geometry.

  • Fill shapes only, 2 to 4 colors per illustration, no outlines, no gradients, rounded corners of 20 percent or more.
  • Illustrations sit on background, never on card, so they read as scenery rather than content.
  • User photos get a 20 px radius and a thin border, no filters.
No glossy stock photosNo before-and-after bodiesNo scales or tape measuresNo 3D renders
Motion motif

Rings fill, numbers count, saves bounce

Motion confirms an action and shows progress. It never decorates. Every animation is under 600 ms and respects reduced-motion settings.

Ring fill500 ms, ease-out, from previous value
0
Quick count-up400 ms, tabular numerals
Gentle bounceSpring on save, 1.12 scale, 300 ms
  • Rings animate from the previous value, never from zero, so a logged snack reads as a small change.
  • Sheets slide up in 250 ms with a standard ease. Nothing bounces except a successful save.
  • No looping animation on any screen the user waits on. Loading uses a ring that sweeps once per second.
